# Words in the app

How the app talks, and the one word it uses for each idea. New copy follows this page.

## Voice

| Trait | Is | Isn't |
|---|---|---|
| Warm | Talks to the two of you, and uses names | Gushy, or cute in an error |
| Playful | A wink in taglines and on a win | A joke when something fails |
| Brief | Buttons of 1 to 4 words, sentences of 14 words or fewer | Paragraphs in a lobby |

Rules are plain. Errors are calm, say what happened and why, and offer the one fix. A win can
be loud.

## One word per idea

| Idea | Say | Don't say |
|---|---|---|
| One play of a game, start to end | **game** ("Game 2" in the header) | battle, match |
| A part of a game (Dominoes, Balloon Words) | **round** | game |
| The other person | **their name**; "the other player" when it isn't known yet | opponent, friend, the host |
| The saved person | **partner** | friend |
| Playing without anyone | **Play solo** (against the computer or alone) | Play alone, Play the computer |
| The code that joins a room | **code**, "room code" nearby | secret code, server link |
| Quitting a game | **Leave** | Exit, Quit |
| Closing a result | **Done** or **Back to games** | Finish |
| Taking back Ready | **Cancel**, with "Waiting for Alex…" | Not ready |
| Someone joining | "Waiting for someone to join…" | Waiting for an opponent |

"Server" appears only in the profile's online settings and the README, for whoever sets up
the online link. Ship Battle keeps its name; inside it a game is still a game.
